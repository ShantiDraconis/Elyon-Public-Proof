# FINAL_FAIL — Minimal Open-Bridge Calculus

Status: NEW FORMAL METAMATHEMATICS / NO PROBLEM-ENDPOINT PROMOTION

Formal source: lean/MCore/FinalFail/MinimalOpenBridge.lean

The FINAL_FAIL audit is promoted from prose/firewall practice to an explicit proof-architecture calculus.

Core result: a MinimalOpenBridge carries Bridge -> Endpoint (certified downstream sufficiency) and Endpoint -> Bridge (indispensability/no certified bypass). Therefore, inside the specified architecture, Endpoint <-> Bridge.

This is architecture-relative. It does not claim that no mathematics outside the architecture can prove the endpoint.

ClosedPrefix records a certified prefix and its implication into the bridge. OpenTerminalChain is the fail-closed ATK state: prefix, downstream implication, indispensability and firewall may be certified while the actual proof of Bridge remains absent. No witness is manufactured.

Firewall fields are noSorry, noAdmit, noOracle, noCircularity and semanticFidelity. The firewall is a prerequisite for promotion and is not used as a mathematical oracle.

For each of the seven chains, the terminal HARD may instantiate Bridge and the official theorem Endpoint. FINAL_PROOF requires the actual bridge proof plus audited maps. FINAL_FAIL/OpenTerminalChain certifies only the architecture-relative reduction Endpoint <-> Bridge, never the unresolved bridge itself.
